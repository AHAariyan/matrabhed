How we work

Four rules we don't bend.

  • Privacy first

    We collect only what a feature needs to work. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

  • On your device

    Where an app can work on your phone alone, it does. Dev Tools, DevDocs and Photobe work offline. When a feature needs a server, such as a NativeToEnglish rewrite, we say so and send only what it needs.

  • Honest pricing

    The price is shown before you buy, with any tax at checkout. Cancel any time. Web purchases can be refunded in full within 14 days, no questions asked.

  • A real person answers

    Every app is supported by the person who wrote it. Questions, bugs and refund requests reach him directly, and replies usually arrive within two business days.

How we sell

Clear about who takes your payment.

Payments are processed by our authorised reseller, Paddle.com, which acts as the merchant of record for web purchases. Purchases made inside an Android app are processed by Google Play. Prices are shown on each product's page before you buy, in your own currency where possible, with any tax shown at checkout.

  • On our websites

    Paddle.com Market Ltd is the merchant of record. It handles the payment, sales tax and receipt, and its name appears on your statement.

  • Inside Android apps

    Google Play processes the purchase under Google's terms, and you manage subscriptions in the Play Store.
